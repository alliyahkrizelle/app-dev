# **Vincenzo**
## **Description:**
> A Korean drama series about an Italian Mafia consigliere of Korean descent who returns to his motherland and uses his ruthless methods to take on a large, corrupt conglomerate.

---
## **Details:**
- **Director:** Kim Hee-won
- **Assistant Director:** Ham Seung-hoon
- **Released Date:** Vincenzo premiered on February 20, 2021, and ran until May 2, 2021
- **Genre:** Crime drama, Black comedy, Romance.
